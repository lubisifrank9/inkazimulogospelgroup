Frequently Asked Questions

Helpful answers about our group, music, programmes and bookings.

What is Inkazimulo Gospel Group?

Inkazimulo Gospel Group is a gospel music and ministry group sharing the message of the Gospel through music and ministry.

Who founded Inkazimulo Gospel Group?

The group was founded by Apostle Elan Mashabane.

Where is Inkazimulo Gospel Group based?

The group is associated with Mangweni, Mpumalanga, South Africa.

What is the main purpose and mission of the group?

The group seeks to share the Gospel, encourage faith and bring people together through gospel music and ministry.

How can I become a member of Inkazimulo Gospel Group?

Use the Contact page to enquire about membership and the group will advise you about the next steps.

Does the group release original gospel music?

Visit the Songs & Albums page for music releases and listening information.

Where can I listen to Inkazimulo Gospel Group's songs?

Check the Songs & Albums page for music platform links. Some platform URLs still need to be supplied.

How can I stay updated about new songs and albums?

Follow the group's official social media pages and check the Songs & Albums page.

Does the group organise gospel programmes and events?

The group participates in gospel programmes and events. Updates can be shared through its official channels.

Can I invite Inkazimulo Gospel Group to perform at my event?

Yes. Complete the Booking form with your event details to send an enquiry.

Does the group travel for performances outside its home area?

The Tours page highlights journeys across South Africa and to neighbouring countries.

Which countries has the group visited for tours?

The supplied website information mentions Mozambique and Eswatini, as well as tours across South Africa.

Does the group offer church services and other activities?

The Church & Other Services page lists the supplied church gatherings and activities.

How can I find out about upcoming programmes and church services?

Check the Programmes & Performances and Church & Other Services pages, and follow official social media updates.

How can I contact Inkazimulo Gospel Group for enquiries or bookings?

Visit the Contact page or submit the online Booking enquiry form.
